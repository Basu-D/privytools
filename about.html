About PrivyTools

Tools About Privacy Policy

PrivyTools is a free, privacy-first collection of file utilities that run entirely in your web browser.

Why this exists

Most online PDF and image tools require you to upload your files to a remote server. That creates privacy risks, slow uploads, file size limits, and accounts you don’t want. PrivyTools does the opposite: all processing happens locally on your device using JavaScript. Your files never leave your computer.

Current tools

  • Merge PDFs
  • Rotate PDF pages
  • Image compress, resize, and convert
  • Images to PDF
  • Base64 encode / decode

How it works

The site uses open-source libraries (mainly pdf-lib) and the browser’s native Canvas API. Nothing is sent to any server.

Support

If the tools are useful, you can support the project via the donation link on the main page. Ads may also appear to help cover hosting and future development — they are clearly marked and do not affect the privacy of your files.
