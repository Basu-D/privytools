Privacy Policy

Tools About Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Your files

PrivyTools processes all files entirely in your web browser. No files are uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server. We never see, receive, or retain your documents or images.

Data we do not collect

  • No accounts or logins
  • No file uploads
  • No tracking of what you process
  • No analytics on the content of your files

Advertisements

This site may display advertisements (for example via Google AdSense or similar networks) to help support development. These ads are served by third-party providers and may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies. The presence of ads does not change the fact that your files stay on your device.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings or through any consent banner the ad network provides.

Third-party services

The site currently loads the pdf-lib library from a public CDN (unpkg). This is a one-time JavaScript file load and does not involve your personal files.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach out via the support / donation links on the main page or through the project’s GitHub repository once it is public.
